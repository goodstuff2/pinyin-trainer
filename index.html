How to play

  1. Words appear one at a time. Remember them.
  2. When a new word shows, spell the pinyin of the word from N cards ago, one letter at a time. Tones are ignored.
  3. A wrong letter or a timeout is a miss. Missed words return a few cards later.
  4. Score 80% or more to level up.
